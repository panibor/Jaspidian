# Code of Conduct

Jaspidian is a small personal-use project. The expectations here are simple:

## Be civil

When opening an issue, commenting on a pull request, or interacting with anyone in this repository:

- Stay on topic — bug reports, feature ideas, technical questions
- Be patient — this is maintained by one person in their spare time
- Be respectful — no harassment, name-calling, doxxing, slurs, or personal attacks
- Be honest — don't misrepresent bugs, exaggerate severity, or post fake reports

## Protect other people's privacy

When sharing examples to demonstrate a bug or feature, **do not include real people's content**:

- Don't paste screenshots showing other people's Facebook posts, comments, names, or profile pictures
- Don't paste captured Markdown notes that include third parties' content
- Scrub identifying information before sharing logs

This applies in issues, PRs, and any discussions.

## Out of bounds

The following are not acceptable in any interaction here:

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats (public or private)
- Discrimination based on identity, beliefs, or background
- Posting other people's private information without their consent
- Spam, advertising, or off-topic promotion

## Enforcement

The maintainer can — at their sole discretion and without prior notice — edit, hide, close, or delete any comment, issue, or pull request; and block any user from further interaction with the repository.

There is no appeal process. This is a personal project, not a democracy.

## Reporting

If someone violates this code, open an issue with the label `conduct` and describe what happened (without naming the reporter publicly, if you prefer). For security-related issues, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

---

This Code of Conduct is intentionally short. It is loosely adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) but kept lightweight for a project of this scale.
